Background

My background is actually in public health, not computer science — I spent four years on a BSc in Public Health before this, including a year as a research associate running longitudinal statistical analysis on wellness outcomes for officer training cohorts. That's part of why I ended up in data science: I was already asking research questions and testing hypotheses, just with SPSS instead of Python.

I'm now doing an MSc in Data Science at the University of Leicester, and the throughline from public health to here is epidemiology and health surveillance — data science applied somewhere the stakes are real, not just a Kaggle leaderboard. I care as much about framing the right question and finding the story in the data as I do about the modeling itself.
